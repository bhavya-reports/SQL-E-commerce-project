A small SQL database for an online retailer and use it to answer real business questions.
This GitHub projectlets you demonstrate:

JOIN
LEFT JOIN
expressions

DELETE

Imagine you're a Junior Data Analyst at an online store called:
NorthStar E-Commerce
The company sells products in different categories and has customers across different countries.
THE database contain:
Customers
    │
    │ customer_id
    ↓
Orders ─────────── Products
    │
    │ order_id
    ↓
Order_Items

Tasks to perform

-- 1.Show all customers.
-- 2. Show customers from Canada.
-- 3.Show all products costing more than $50.
-- 4.Show products in the Electronics category.
-- 5.Display the 10 most expensive products.
-- 6.Show all completed orders.
-- 7.Show customers who signed up after January 1, 2025.
-- 8.Show each order with: customer name, order(id,date,status)
-- 9. Show every product that has been ordered (product name, category, quantity ordered)
-- 10. Show all customers and their orders, including customers who have never placed an order.
-- 11. Show each order with :( customer name, product name, quantity, price) 
-- 12. Calculate the revenue generated by each order item.
-- 13. Calculate the total revenue generated by the store.
-- 14. Calculate total revenue for each product.
-- 15. Calculate total quantity sold for each product.
-- 16. Find the top 10 best-selling products based on quantity.
-- 17. Find the top 10 products by revenue.
-- 18. Calculate average order value.
-- 19. Calculate total revenue by country.
-- 20. Find how many customers are from each country.
-- 21. Calculate average product price by category.
-- 22. Find categories generating more than $5,000 in revenue.
-- 23. Find the top 10 customers by total spending.
-- 24. Find customers who have never placed an order.
-- 25. Find customers who have placed more than 3 orders.
-- 26.Find the average amount spent per customer.
-- 27.Find the country with the highest total revenue.
-- 28.Which product generates the most revenue?
-- 29.Which category generates the most revenue?
-- 30.Which country generates the most revenue?
-- 31.Who is the most valuable customer?
-- 32.Which products have never been sold?
-- 33.Which products have less than 20 units in stock?
-- 34.Which products are both expensive and low-stock?
-- 35. What percentage of orders are cancelled?
-- 36.Find the customer who has spent the most money.
-- 37.Find the second-highest revenue-generating product.
-- 38.Find the best-selling product in each category.
-- 39.Find the country with the highest average order value.
